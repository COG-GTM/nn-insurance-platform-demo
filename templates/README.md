# Templates

## NN-presentation-template.pptx

NN-branded PowerPoint template, 16:9 widescreen:

- **Slide 1 — title:** NN logo, title, subtitle, presenter line.
- **Slide 2 — standard content:** title with header rule, section header, bullets, body paragraph, and a tinted highlight card for a stat or quote.

Palette: NN orange `#EE7F00`, deep orange `#E35205`, ink `#3C3C3B`, grey `#6E7377`, card tint `#FDF4EA`, white background. Arial throughout so it renders identically on any Office install.

Duplicate slide 2 for each new section.
